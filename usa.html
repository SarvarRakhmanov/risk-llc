B-2 tourist visa (B1/B2)

Who it's for

Tourists, people visiting family or friends, and those coming for medical treatment.

Main requirements

  • Valid passport and DS-160 confirmation
  • Visa fee payment and interview at a US embassy or consulate
  • Proof of strong ties to your home country (job, family, property)
  • Enough funds for the trip

Typical processing time

Typically decided at the interview, with the passport returned within days to a few weeks. Interview wait times vary greatly by embassy, from weeks to many months; see travel.state.gov.

How RISK helps

We assess your chances honestly, complete the DS-160, book the appointment, organize documents and run mock interviews in Russian, Uzbek or English. We also help after a 214(b) refusal.

ESTA / Visa Waiver (for eligible countries)

Who it's for

Citizens of Visa Waiver Program countries visiting for up to 90 days.

Main requirements

  • Eligible passport (e-passport)
  • Online ESTA application
  • No previous immigration violations

Typical processing time

Typically approved within 72 hours (often minutes).

How RISK helps

We check whether ESTA or a B visa is right for you.

Business

Short business trips, conferences, negotiations and trade shows.

B-1 business visitor visa

Who it's for

Business people attending meetings, conferences, trainings or negotiating contracts (no paid local work).

Main requirements

  • DS-160 and interview
  • Invitation letter or conference registration
  • Employer letter and proof of business ties

Typical processing time

Same as B-2: typically decided at the interview; appointment wait times vary by country.

How RISK helps

We prepare invitation and cover letters, organize evidence of your business and coach you for the interview.

Student

Study at US universities, colleges, language schools or exchange programs. Студенческая виза США.

F-1 student visa

Who it's for

Students admitted to a SEVP-certified school, university or language program.

Main requirements

  • Form I-20 from the school
  • SEVIS I-901 fee paid
  • Proof of funds for tuition and living costs
  • Academic records and intent to return

Typical processing time

Typically decided at the interview; student visas can be issued up to 365 days before the program start. Book early in spring/summer.

How RISK helps

University selection and admission support, financial documents and sponsor letters, DS-160 and interview coaching.

J-1 exchange visitor visa

Who it's for

Work & Travel students, interns, trainees, researchers, au pairs and exchange teachers.

Main requirements

  • Form DS-2019 from a designated sponsor
  • SEVIS fee
  • Program-specific funds and English level

Typical processing time

Typically decided at the interview after the sponsor issues the DS-2019.

How RISK helps

We help choose a sponsor, prepare documents and practice the interview.

M-1 vocational student visa

Who it's for

Students of vocational and technical programs.

Main requirements

  • Form I-20 (M)
  • Proof of full tuition and living funds

Typical processing time

Typically decided at the interview.

How RISK helps

Program selection and full application support.

Work

Temporary work visas for specialists and transferred employees.

H-1B specialty occupation

Who it's for

Professionals with at least a bachelor's degree and a US employer offer in a specialty occupation.

Main requirements

  • US employer sponsor and annual registration/lottery (cap cases)
  • Labor Condition Application
  • Degree or equivalent experience

Typical processing time

Registration is in March; petitions are typically decided in a few months by USCIS, or within 15 business days with premium processing.

How RISK helps

We help employers and candidates organize the petition evidence and the consular stage.

L-1 intracompany transferee

Who it's for

Managers, executives and specialists of a company with offices abroad and in the US.

Main requirements

  • At least 1 year with the related company abroad in the last 3 years
  • Qualifying corporate relationship

Typical processing time

Typically a few months at USCIS; premium processing is available.

How RISK helps

Evidence of the company relationship and the role, plus consular interview preparation.

O-1 extraordinary ability

Who it's for

People with extraordinary ability in science, business, arts, education or athletics.

Main requirements

  • Evidence such as awards, publications, high salary, media coverage, judging
  • US employer or agent petition

Typical processing time

Typically a few months; premium processing is available.

How RISK helps

We build a strong evidence portfolio and recommendation letters.

Immigration / PR / Green card

Green cards (permanent residence) through family, employment, the DV lottery, and cases inside the US. Грин карта · Green karta.

DV lottery 2027 (Diversity Visa green card)

Who it's for

Natives of eligible countries (including Uzbekistan) with a high school education or 2 years of qualifying work experience.

Main requirements

  • Free online entry at dvprogram.state.gov during the registration period (usually October–November)
  • Correct recent photo
  • If selected: DS-260, civil documents, medical exam and interview

Typical processing time

Results are typically available from May the following year; interviews for selectees run during the fiscal year starting October 1.

How RISK helps

Correct photo and entry, saving your confirmation number, then full support for selectees: DS-260, documents and interview preparation.

Family-based green card

Who it's for

Spouses, children, parents and siblings of US citizens, and spouses/children of permanent residents.

Main requirements

  • I-130 petition by the US relative
  • Affidavit of support (I-864)
  • Civil documents and medical exam

Typical processing time

Typically from about a year for immediate relatives to many years for preference categories, depending on the Visa Bulletin.

How RISK helps

We organize the petition, NVC documents and interview preparation.

K-1 fiancé(e) visa

Who it's for

Fiancé(e)s of US citizens planning to marry within 90 days of arrival.

Main requirements

  • I-129F petition
  • Proof of a genuine relationship and an in-person meeting within 2 years

Typical processing time

Typically many months to over a year in total.

How RISK helps

Relationship evidence, forms and interview coaching.

Employment-based green cards (EB-1, EB-2 NIW, EB-3, EB-5)

Who it's for

Workers with US employer sponsorship, people of exceptional ability (NIW) and investors (EB-5).

Main requirements

  • PERM labor certification for many EB-2/EB-3 cases
  • I-140 petition
  • EB-5: qualifying investment

Typical processing time

Typically from about a year to several years, depending on category and country.

How RISK helps

Category selection, evidence planning and document preparation.

Immigration cases inside the USA

Who it's for

People already in the US who need to adjust or maintain status.

Main requirements

  • Adjustment of status (I-485)
  • RFE (Request for Evidence) responses
  • Extension or change of status (I-539), e.g. B-2 → F-1
  • Work permit (EAD) and travel document

Typical processing time

Typically several months to over a year; check uscis.gov processing times.

How RISK helps

We organize your filing, draft responses to RFEs and keep deadlines.

Processing times are general and change often. Always check the current official estimate: travel.state.gov · uscis.gov
