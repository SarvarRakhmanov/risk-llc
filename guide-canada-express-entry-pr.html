Canada Express Entry PR explained

Last updated: 2026-10-11 · Checked Oct 2026 on official government websites.

Short answer

Express Entry is Canada's online system for skilled-worker permanent residence through the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Canadian Experience Class and the Federal Skilled Trades Program. You create a profile, get a Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score, and the highest-ranked candidates receive an invitation to apply for PR. After you become a PR, you can apply for Canadian citizenship once you have been physically present for at least 1,095 days in the last 5 years.

Step by step

  1. Take an approved English or French test and, for foreign degrees, get an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA).
  2. Check which program you qualify for and find your job's NOC code.
  3. Create an Express Entry profile; you receive a CRS score and enter the pool.
  4. Wait for an invitation to apply (ITA) in a draw; improve your score meanwhile (language, education, Canadian experience, provincial nomination).
  5. After an ITA, submit the full PR application with documents and fees, usually within 60 days.
  6. Complete biometrics and medicals, receive confirmation of PR and land in Canada.

Documents checklist

  • Passport
  • Language test results (IELTS/CELPIP/PTE Core or TEF/TCF)
  • ECA report for foreign education
  • Reference letters describing duties, hours and salary
  • Proof of funds bank letters (unless exempt)
  • Police certificates and medical exam
  • Job offer or provincial nomination, if you have one

Fees (official)

  • PR application incl. right of PR fee: CAD 1,590 per adult (CAD 990 + CAD 600)
  • Dependent child: CAD 270
  • Biometrics: CAD 85 (CAD 170 per family)
  • Proof of funds (FSW/FST, updated July 2025): CAD 15,263 for 1 person, CAD 19,001 for 2, CAD 28,362 for 4
  • Citizenship application later: CAD 653 per adult

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a job offer for Express Entry?

No. A job offer is not required for most candidates, and since March 25, 2025 job offers no longer add CRS points. A provincial nomination still adds a large number of points.

Who does not need proof of funds?

Candidates invited under the Canadian Experience Class, and those authorized to work in Canada with a valid job offer.

What is the minimum CRS score?

There is no fixed minimum. Each draw has its own cut-off, which changes from draw to draw and by category. Check recent rounds on canada.ca.

How soon can I become a Canadian citizen?

After becoming a permanent resident, you need at least 1,095 days of physical presence in Canada in the 5 years before you apply, plus tax filing and language and knowledge requirements where they apply.
