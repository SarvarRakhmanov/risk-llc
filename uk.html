Tourist / Visitor

Visit the UK for tourism or family. Виза в Великобританию · Angliya vizasi.

Standard Visitor visa

Who it's for

Tourists, family visitors and people attending short events, for up to 6 months per visit.

Main requirements

  • Online application and biometrics at a visa centre
  • Proof of funds and accommodation
  • Intention to leave the UK

Typical processing time

Typically a decision within 3 weeks of the biometrics appointment (gov.uk).

How RISK helps

Application and cover letter, document review and evidence of ties. Long-term 2, 5 and 10 year visas available.

Business

Short business trips to the UK.

Standard Visitor (business activities)

Who it's for

People attending meetings, conferences, negotiations or training.

Main requirements

  • Invitation from the UK company or event
  • Employer letter

Typical processing time

Typically within 3 weeks.

How RISK helps

Business letters and evidence preparation.

Student

Study at a UK university. Angliya talaba vizasi · студенческая виза в Великобританию.

Student visa

Who it's for

Students with an offer from a licensed student sponsor.

Main requirements

  • Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)
  • Proof of funds held for 28 days
  • English level (e.g. IELTS UKVI) and TB test if required

Typical processing time

Typically a decision within 3 weeks when applying from outside the UK (gov.uk).

How RISK helps

University choice and admission, funds and 28-day rule check, credibility interview preparation.

Work

Work in the UK.

Skilled Worker visa

Who it's for

Workers with a job offer from a UK licensed sponsor.

Main requirements

  • Certificate of Sponsorship
  • Meeting salary and skill thresholds
  • English requirement

Typical processing time

Typically within 3 weeks from outside the UK.

How RISK helps

Checking your job offer and sponsor, and preparing the application.

Processing times are general and change often. Always check the current official estimate: gov.uk visa processing times
