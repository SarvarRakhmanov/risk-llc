Explainer: how a Canada work permit works for applicants from Uzbekistan

October 11, 2026 · Canada has two kinds of work permits, employer-specific and open. Most applicants from Uzbekistan need a job offer, pay CAD 155 plus CAD 85 biometrics, and can later move to PR and citizenship.

Two types. An employer-specific work permit needs a job offer; an open work permit does not, but is available only to specific groups (for example some spouses and graduates of Canadian schools). Uzbekistan is not part of International Experience Canada, so that youth route is not available.

Requirements outside Canada (IRCC Guide 5487): show you will leave at the end of your employment, have enough money for yourself, your family and the trip home, be law-abiding (a police certificate may be requested), not be a security risk, have a medical exam if needed, and include the LMIA copy and employment contract when an LMIA is required.

Fees: work permit CAD 155, open work permit holder fee CAD 100, biometrics CAD 85.

Processing time depends on the country you apply from and is updated weekly; check IRCC's official tool before planning travel.

Path to PR and citizenship: after at least 1 year (1,560 hours) of skilled paid work in Canada within 3 years, you may qualify for the Canadian Experience Class through Express Entry (PR application CAD 1,590 incl. right of PR fee), or a Provincial Nominee Program. As a PR you can apply for citizenship after 1,095 days of physical presence in 5 years.

Scam warning: real Canadian employers do not sell job offers or LMIAs. Use Job Bank and check that any paid representative is authorized.
