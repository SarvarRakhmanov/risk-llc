Visa & Immigration News

Latest US, Canada and UK visa and immigration updates, checked on official government sources.

  • Express Entry from 3 November 2026: extra CRS points for high-wage jobs, no LMIA for job offersOctober 21, 2026 · On 16 October 2026 IRCC announced that from 3 November 2026 Express Entry candidates get extra CRS points for Canadian work experience or a job offer in a high-wage occupation, and a job offer no longer needs an LMIA. Invitation rounds pause for up to two weeks while scores are recalculated.
  • USA: no more automatic EAD extensions, DHS final rule in force from 14 October 2026October 21, 2026 · On 14 October 2026 DHS published a final rule confirming that work permits (EADs) are no longer automatically extended while a renewal is pending. Renewals filed on or after 30 October 2025 get no extension: the card expires on its printed date. File the renewal as soon as you are within 180 days of expiry.
  • Proposal: USCIS wants to stop deferring to prior approvals when extending H-1B, L-1 and other work visasOctober 21, 2026 · On 14 October 2026 USCIS published a proposed rule to remove 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5), the regulation that tells officers to defer to an earlier approval when deciding an extension petition. It is only a proposal: comments are open until 13 November 2026, and nothing changes until a final rule is published.
  • New client results: UK visitor visa and Japan eVisa approvedOctober 21, 2026 · On 19 October 2026 two RISK LLC clients from Uzbekistan received approvals: a UK Standard Visitor visa and a Japan temporary visitor eVisa.
  • DV-2027 green card lottery: court ordered registration (not open yet)October 16, 2026 · On October 14, 2026 Judge Jinsook Ohta ordered the State Department to run DV-2027. After a same-day joint modification, the department must announce by October 29 and open registration by November 1, 2026 (US Eastern), for 30 days. Registration is still not open.
  • UK Student visa: money you must show rises from 30 November 2026October 11, 2026 · Under HC 584, UK Student visa applicants must show £1,570 a month in London and £1,203 a month outside London for applications made on or after 30 November 2026.
  • Explainer: how a Canada work permit works for applicants from UzbekistanOctober 11, 2026 · Canada has two kinds of work permits, employer-specific and open. Most applicants from Uzbekistan need a job offer, pay CAD 155 plus CAD 85 biometrics, and can later move to PR and citizenship.
