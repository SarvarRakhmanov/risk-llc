UK earned settlement: ILR after 10 years by default, 5 years from £50,271, 3 years from £125,141

November 9, 2026 · On 2 November 2026 the UK government published its final earned settlement policy: the standard wait for indefinite leave to remain (ILR) rises from 5 to 10 years, with 5 years for people earning at least £50,271 and 3 years from £125,141. The Immigration Rules and start date have not been published yet.

What happened (policy final, rules not yet published): on 2 November 2026 the Home Secretary presented the government's response to the earned settlement consultation to Parliament. The consultation received 204,667 responses. Settlement (indefinite leave to remain, ILR) will no longer come almost automatically after 5 years. The default qualifying period becomes 10 years. People who contribute more can settle faster, and people who break the rules wait longer. The government says it will first publish detailed rules, technical guidance and an impact assessment. No start date has been announced.

The new pathways: 3 years for earnings of at least £125,141 a year (in 3 of the last 4 years) and for top talent routes. 5 years for earnings of at least £50,271 (in 3 of the last 5 years) and for public service roles such as doctors, nurses and teachers. 10 years as the default, with earnings of at least £12,571 in 5 of the last 7 years. Specified immigration breaches, such as overstaying, push settlement back by up to 20 years.

Requirements for everyone: good character with no criminal record, no unpaid debt to the government, B2 English, the Life in the UK test, and sustained contribution. Higher earnings cannot offset a criminal record.

Students and graduates: the Long Residence route will close. Time on the Student, Graduate, High Potential Individual, Visit, Seasonal Worker and other temporary routes will no longer count towards settlement. Time on Skilled Worker, Global Talent, Innovator Founder, family partner routes and other core routes still counts.

Transition: care workers who entered or switched into the route before the May 2025 White Paper keep a 5-year path if they meet the £25,000 salary and sponsorship conditions. Those who do not can apply for a further 5-year care visa. From 1 February 2027, care workers whose sponsor loses its licence get up to 120 days to find a new job offer. Families of British citizens, BN(O) holders and armed forces members keep their current routes. Anyone who already holds ILR keeps it.

What to do now: rules apply at the date you apply, not the date you arrived. Skilled Worker close to 5 years? Check your salary history and English level now.
