Express Entry from 3 November 2026: extra CRS points for high-wage jobs, no LMIA for job offers

October 21, 2026 · On 16 October 2026 IRCC announced that from 3 November 2026 Express Entry candidates get extra CRS points for Canadian work experience or a job offer in a high-wage occupation, and a job offer no longer needs an LMIA. Invitation rounds pause for up to two weeks while scores are recalculated.

What changed (official, final): on October 16, 2026 Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) announced changes to the Express Entry Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS). From November 3, 2026 candidates get extra points for eligible Canadian work experience or a qualifying job offer in a high-wage occupation. From the same date a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) is no longer required for a job offer, for the points or for Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades eligibility.

What counts as high-wage: an occupation (NOC code) whose Canada-wide median wage is at least 1.3 times the median wage of the whole Canadian workforce. IRCC looks at the occupation, not at your personal salary. The official list (in force from November 3, 2026, based on 2023 to 2025 wage data) has three levels: 2 times, 1.5 times and 1.3 times the median.

How many points: at least 12 months of Canadian work experience in a high-wage occupation adds 70 points (2 times the median), 40 (1.5 times) or 20 (1.3 times) on top of the existing points for length of Canadian experience. A qualifying high-wage job offer gives 100, 70 or 50 points for the same levels. Not combined: you get the higher one. The factor is capped at 150 points without an accompanying spouse, 140 with one.

Job offer rules: full-time, continuous work in Canada for at least one year after you become a permanent resident. An officer must find the offer genuine: an operating employer with a real need that can pay and follows employment law. Extra points do not guarantee an invitation; each round keeps its own requirements and CRS cut-off.

Already in the pool? Points for Canadian work experience are recalculated automatically. For job-offer points, add the offer to your profile before November 3, 2026. IRCC says to answer 'Yes' to the LMIA question and enter LMIA number 00000, dates 2026-01-01 and 2029-01-01. Recalculation may take up to two weeks; invitation rounds are paused until it ends.

For applicants from Uzbekistan: the new points go to people already working in Canada or holding a Canadian job offer. Applying from abroad without Canadian experience, your CRS score does not change while in-Canada candidates move up. The usual route stays: a work permit or study permit first, then Express Entry through the Canadian Experience Class. Questions?
