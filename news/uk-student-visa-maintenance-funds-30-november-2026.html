UK Student visa: money you must show rises from 30 November 2026

October 11, 2026 · Under HC 584, UK Student visa applicants must show £1,570 a month in London and £1,203 a month outside London for applications made on or after 30 November 2026.

The UK Home Office is raising the maintenance (living cost) money that Student visa applicants must show. The change comes from the Statement of Changes to the Immigration Rules HC 584, laid on 3 September 2026, and applies to applications made on or after 30 November 2026.

New amounts per month: London £1,570 (was £1,529); outside London £1,203 (was £1,171).

Maximum for 9 months: London £14,130 (was £13,761); outside London £10,827 (was £10,539). The cap for offsetting accommodation fees already paid rises to £1,570.

Applications made before 30 November 2026 are decided under the rules in force on 29 November, so the old amounts apply to them.

What to do: if you plan to apply in late November or later, make sure your bank statements cover the new amounts for the required period. If you are ready earlier, applying before 30 November keeps the old amounts.
