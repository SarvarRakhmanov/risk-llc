DV-2027 green card lottery: court ordered registration (not open yet)

October 16, 2026 · On October 14, 2026 Judge Jinsook Ohta ordered the State Department to run DV-2027. After a same-day joint modification, the department must announce by October 29 and open registration by November 1, 2026 (US Eastern), for 30 days. Registration is still not open.

Court order (Oct 14, 2026): in Red Eagle Law, L.C. v. Rubio (S.D. Cal.), Judge Jinsook Ohta granted a preliminary injunction requiring the State Department to announce and open the fiscal year 2027 Diversity Visa registration period, keep it open for 30 days, then notify selectees and process DV-2027 visas through September 30, 2027 or until the visa allocation is exhausted.

Modified deadlines (same day): both parties jointly asked to move the original dates. The court set announcement by midnight Eastern October 29, 2026, registration opening by midnight Eastern November 1, 2026, and selectee notification by midnight Eastern December 3, 2026.

Status today (Oct 16, 2026): registration is not open yet. Exact opening and closing times will come only from the official State Department announcement on travel.state.gov and the Federal Register. Check dvprogram.state.gov when they publish dates. Nobody can register you early.

Final (official): the lottery was not abolished. The Diversity Visa program is set by US law; only Congress can repeal it. Earlier administrative pauses are separate from this registration order.

Entry rules already in force: a $1 fee at entry (Pay.gov), plus a valid passport and bio-page scan for the principal entrant, with strict photo rules (600×600 JPEG, white background, no glasses, photo within 6 months).

Be careful: enter only on the official site when it opens. Selection is random; nobody can guarantee a win. This is general information, not a promise that registration will open on a specific clock hour.
