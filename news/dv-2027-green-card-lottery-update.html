DV-2027 green card lottery: registration still not open (October 2026 update)

October 11, 2026 · As of October 11, 2026 the State Department has not opened DV-2027 registration. The December 2025 pause on DV visa issuance was lifted on August 28, 2026 by court order in Medani v. Trump, and further court dates are set for October 14–15.

Final (official): DV-2027 registration has not opened. On November 5, 2025 the State Department said it is changing the DV entry process and will announce the DV-2027 registration dates "as soon as practicable". As of October 11, 2026 that page has not been updated and no dates have been announced. The DV-2027 visa application period stays October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027.

Final (official): the lottery was not abolished. The Diversity Visa program is set by US law, and only Congress can repeal it. What changed were administrative pauses.

What happened: in late December 2025 the State Department paused all DV visa issuance after the Brown University and MIT shootings, and USCIS put DV green card cases inside the US on hold. On January 21, 2026 a separate pause stopped immigrant visas for nationals of 75 countries, including Uzbekistan; a federal court in New York vacated that pause on August 21, 2026.

Court order (temporary): on August 28, 2026, in Medani v. Trump (N.D. Cal.), the judge temporarily vacated the DV pauses pending further litigation. The State Department confirms that the December 2025 pause "is no longer in effect". This is a preliminary ruling, not a final judgment.

Pending: according to court filings, a ruling in a separate lawsuit seeking to force DV-2027 registration (Red Eagle Law v. Rubio) is expected on October 14, 2026, and a Medani status conference is set for October 15, 2026. Outcomes are not known yet.

New entry rules already in force for the next lottery: a $1 registration fee and, since April 10, 2026, a valid passport and a scan of its bio page are required at entry, with stricter photo rules.

Be careful: registration happens only on the official dvprogram.state.gov site when the State Department announces it. Nobody can register you early or guarantee a win.
