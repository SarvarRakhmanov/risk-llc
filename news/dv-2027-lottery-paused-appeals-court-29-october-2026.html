DV-2027 green card lottery: appeals court paused the order, registration did not open on 1 November 2026

November 2, 2026 · On October 29, 2026 the US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit stayed the district court order that required the State Department to open DV-2027 registration by November 1. As of November 2, 2026 registration is not open and there is no opening date. The program is not abolished; the appeal continues.

Status on November 2, 2026 (verified): DV-2027 registration is not open and no opening date exists. On October 29, 2026 the US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit (case 26-6943) granted the State Department's emergency motion and stayed the preliminary injunction that had required the Department to announce the DV-2027 registration period by October 29 and open it by November 1, 2026. While the stay is in force, those deadlines do not apply.

Why the court paused the order: the three-judge panel (McKeown, N.R. Smith, Thomas) found that the government is likely to win the appeal because the only plaintiff, the law firm Red Eagle Law, L.C., is not within the "zone of interests" of the Diversity Visa statute: no prospective entrants were parties. The panel wrote that it does not endorse the Department's failure to open registration, but the firm has no cause of action. Judge Smith added that the firm also lacks standing.

What happens next: the stay does not end the case. The government's opening brief is due November 17, 2026 and Red Eagle's answer December 15, 2026. The firm may ask the full court to reconsider. The Diversity Visa program itself stays in US law (8 U.S.C. 1153(c)); only Congress can repeal it. In practice, however, the DV-2027 lottery has no date, and any registration would be announced only by the State Department.

What we checked: on November 2, 2026 the official entry site dvprogram.state.gov still shows the DV-2026 text: "Entries are not being accepted at this time". The travel.state.gov visa newsroom has no DV-2027 announcement (its newest item is October 2, 2026), and there is no Federal Register notice. Our October 16 article described the district court order; this article replaces its timeline.

Beware of scams: anyone offering to "register you for DV-2027" today is lying. Entries can be filed only on dvprogram.state.gov during an officially announced period, and the State Department advises entrants to fill in the form themselves, without agents. Do not pay for a lottery that is not open.
