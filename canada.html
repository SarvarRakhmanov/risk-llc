Visitor visa (TRV)

Who it's for

Tourists and family visitors from visa-required countries.

Main requirements

  • Online IRCC application and biometrics
  • Proof of funds and travel history
  • Ties to home country and purpose of trip

Typical processing time

Typically from a few weeks to a few months, depending on the country you apply from; see the canada.ca processing times tool.

How RISK helps

Strong letters of explanation, invitation letters, document checklist and full online application. Many clients receive multiple-entry visas.

Super visa

Who it's for

Parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and permanent residents.

Main requirements

  • Invitation and minimum income of the host
  • Private Canadian medical insurance for at least 1 year
  • Medical exam

Typical processing time

Typically several months; check canada.ca.

How RISK helps

Income documents, insurance guidance and full application.

Business

Short business visits to Canada.

Business visitor

Who it's for

People attending meetings, conferences and trade fairs without entering the Canadian labour market.

Main requirements

  • Visitor visa or eTA
  • Invitation or conference letter
  • Employer support letter

Typical processing time

Same as the visitor visa: typically weeks to months.

How RISK helps

Business-purpose letters and evidence of employment and ties.

Student

Study at a Canadian college or university.

Study permit

Who it's for

International students accepted by a Designated Learning Institution (DLI).

Main requirements

  • Letter of acceptance and provincial attestation letter (PAL) where required
  • Proof of funds (tuition + living costs)
  • Biometrics and medical exam if required

Typical processing time

Typically several weeks to a few months; apply early.

How RISK helps

School selection, PAL, funds planning and a convincing study plan.

Typically weeks to several months.

Immigration / PR / Green card

Permanent residence in Canada.

Express Entry PR

Who it's for

Skilled workers (FSW, CEC, FST) with good language scores, education and experience.

Main requirements

  • Language test (IELTS/CELPIP/TEF)
  • Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)
  • Express Entry profile and an Invitation to Apply (ITA)

Typical processing time

Most complete applications are typically processed in about 6 months after the ITA (official service standard).

How RISK helps

CRS score calculation and improvement plan, profile creation and the full PR application after your ITA.

Provincial Nominee Program (PNP)

Who it's for

Candidates who match a province's labour or business needs.

Main requirements

  • Provincial nomination
  • Meeting provincial stream criteria

Typical processing time

Typically longer than Express Entry for paper-based streams; check canada.ca.

How RISK helps

Matching your profile to provincial streams and preparing applications.

Family sponsorship

Who it's for

Spouses, partners, dependent children, parents and grandparents of Canadians/PRs.

Main requirements

  • Sponsor eligibility
  • Proof of a genuine relationship

Typical processing time

Spousal sponsorship is typically about a year.

How RISK helps

Relationship evidence and complete application package.

Canada PR (Express Entry): required documents

Based on the official IRCC lists. Your personal document checklist in your IRCC account is final.

  • A valid passport or travel document (renew it if it expires within 6 months)
  • Language test results
  • An Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) report, or proof of Canadian education (required for the Federal Skilled Worker Program, or to get points for education outside Canada)
  • Proof of work experience, such as reference or experience letters from employers
  • A written job offer from a Canadian employer (if you have one)
  • A certificate of qualification in a trade from a Canadian province or territory (if you have one)
  • Proof of funds: official bank letters (not needed for the Canadian Experience Class, or with a valid job offer and authorization to work in Canada)
  • Police certificates
  • An immigration medical exam by a panel physician
  • A provincial nomination (if you have one)
  • Civil documents as they apply: birth certificates of dependent children, marriage or divorce certificates, common-law union form (IMM 5409)
  • Certified English or French translations of documents in other languages

Canada PR FAQ

What is Express Entry?

An online system IRCC uses to manage applications for 3 programs: the Canadian Experience Class, the Federal Skilled Worker Program and the Federal Skilled Trades Program. If you are eligible for a Provincial Nominee Program, you can also apply through Express Entry.

What language level do I need?

It depends on the program: for example, CLB 7 for the Federal Skilled Worker Program; CLB 7 or CLB 5 for the Canadian Experience Class depending on the occupation's TEER category; and CLB 5 for speaking and listening and CLB 4 for reading and writing for the Federal Skilled Trades Program.

How much work experience do I need?

Canadian Experience Class: 1 year in Canada in the last 3 years. Federal Skilled Worker Program: 1 year continuous within the last 10 years. Federal Skilled Trades Program: 2 years within the last 5 years.

What happens after I get an Invitation to Apply (ITA)?

The invitation is valid for 60 days. You fill out the permanent residence application, upload your documents, get the medical exam, pay the fees and submit. If you are not invited within 12 months, your profile expires.

How much money do I need as proof of funds?

The minimum depends on your family size and is updated every year. The bank letters must show your account details, current balances and the average balance for the past 6 months. Check the current table on canada.ca before you apply.

How long does processing take?

IRCC aims to process most complete Express Entry applications in about 6 months (its official service standard). Check the current processing times on canada.ca.
