USA: F-1 student visa (I-20)

Who it is for

Bachelor's, master's, English language training (ESL) and community college programs at SEVP-certified schools.

Main steps

  1. Choose a program and get admission
  2. Receive Form I-20 from the school and pay the SEVIS I-901 fee
  3. Show funds for tuition and living costs (personal or sponsor)
  4. Fill the DS-160, book the appointment and attend the interview

How RISK helps

Community college 2+2 transfer plans, ESL schools in New York and California, university shortlists, I-20 and financial documents, DS-160 and mock F-1 interviews.

Canada: study permit

Who it is for

College diplomas, bachelor's and master's programs and language schools at Designated Learning Institutions (DLIs).

Main steps

  1. Letter of acceptance (LOA) from a DLI
  2. Provincial attestation letter (PAL) where required
  3. Proof of funds, study plan and biometrics
  4. Online IRCC application

How RISK helps

School and program choice, LOA and PAL, funds planning and a strong study plan.

United Kingdom: Student visa

Who it is for

Bachelor's, master's and pre-sessional English courses at licensed student sponsors.

Main steps

  1. Offer and Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)
  2. English level (for example IELTS for UKVI) where required
  3. Funds held for 28 days
  4. Online application, biometrics and TB test if required

How RISK helps

University admission, CAS, the 28-day funds rule and credibility interview preparation.

Japan: student visa

Who it is for

Japanese language schools, vocational schools and universities.

Main steps

  1. Admission and a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) through the school
  2. Proof of funds
  3. Visa application at the embassy or consulate

How RISK helps

School selection and COE document preparation.

Australia: Student visa (subclass 500)

Who it is for

Universities, vocational (VET) and English (ELICOS) courses.

Main steps

  1. Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
  2. Genuine student requirement, funds and health insurance (OSHC)
  3. Online ImmiAccount application

How RISK helps

Course selection, CoE, genuine student statement and documents.
