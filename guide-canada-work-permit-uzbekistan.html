How to get a Canada work permit from Uzbekistan

Last updated: 2026-10-11 · Checked Oct 2026 on official government websites.

Short answer

Most citizens of Uzbekistan get a Canada work permit by first receiving a genuine job offer from a Canadian employer, usually supported by a positive Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) or an LMIA-exempt offer number. You then apply online to IRCC, pay the CAD 155 work permit fee plus CAD 85 for biometrics, and give fingerprints at a visa application centre. Real employers never charge you for a job offer.

Step by step

  1. Find a real Canadian employer in your field (Job Bank, company career pages, recruiters licensed in Canada).
  2. The employer gets a positive LMIA from Employment and Social Development Canada, or submits an LMIA-exempt offer through the Employer Portal and pays the CAD 230 compliance fee.
  3. You receive a signed job offer and the LMIA number or offer of employment number.
  4. Create an IRCC secure account and submit the employer-specific work permit application with all documents.
  5. Pay fees, then give biometrics after you receive the instruction letter; do a medical exam or police certificate if asked.
  6. If approved, you get a port-of-entry letter (and a visitor visa); the work permit itself is issued by the border officer when you arrive.

Documents checklist

  • Valid passport
  • Signed job offer / employment contract
  • LMIA copy or offer of employment number
  • Diplomas, licences and reference letters proving your experience
  • Language test results if the job requires them
  • Police certificate and medical exam (if requested)
  • Digital photo and proof of funds for your first weeks in Canada

Fees (official)

  • Work permit: CAD 155 per person
  • Biometrics: CAD 85 (CAD 170 for a family)
  • Open work permit holder fee (e.g. eligible spouses): CAD 100
  • Employer compliance fee (paid by the employer, not you): CAD 230

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a Canada work permit without a job offer?

Usually not. Open work permits exist only for specific groups, such as eligible spouses of some workers and students or graduates of Canadian schools. Most people from Uzbekistan need an employer-specific permit based on a job offer.

How long does a Canada work permit take?

It depends on the country you apply from and changes weekly. Check the current time for Uzbekistan in IRCC's official processing times tool before you plan your trip.

Can a work permit lead to Canada PR?

Yes. After at least 1 year (1,560 hours) of skilled paid work in Canada within 3 years, you may qualify for the Canadian Experience Class through Express Entry, or for a Provincial Nominee Program.

Someone offers me a Canadian job for money. Is it real?

Be careful. IRCC warns that job offer scams are common. Employers must not charge you for an LMIA or a job offer.
