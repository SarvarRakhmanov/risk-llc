Canada visitor visa: requirements and documents

Last updated: 2026-10-11 · Checked Oct 2026 on official government websites.

Short answer

Citizens of Uzbekistan need a Canada visitor visa (temporary resident visa) to visit. You apply online, pay CAD 100 plus CAD 85 for biometrics, and must show a clear purpose of travel, enough money for the trip and strong ties that will bring you home. The border officer decides how long you may stay, usually up to 6 months.

Step by step

  1. Check you need a visa and choose the visitor visa application in your IRCC account.
  2. Collect documents: passport, finances, employment and purpose of travel.
  3. Fill in the forms, including the family information form, and upload scans.
  4. Pay CAD 100 + CAD 85 biometrics, submit, and book biometrics at a visa application centre.
  5. If asked, send your passport for the visa counterfoil; then travel and answer the border officer's questions.

Documents checklist

  • Passport valid for your trip, plus copies of old visas
  • Digital photo
  • Bank statements (usually several months) and proof of income
  • Employment letter with position, salary and approved leave, or business registration
  • Travel plan and hotel booking (do not buy final tickets before the visa)
  • Invitation letter and host's status proof if visiting family or friends
  • Family information form (IMM 5707)

Fees (official)

  • Visitor visa: CAD 100 per person (max CAD 500 per family of 5+)
  • Biometrics: CAD 85 per person, CAD 170 per family
  • Biometrics are generally valid for 10 years

Official sources

  • Visit Canada (IRCC) (www.canada.ca)

  • Biometrics (www.canada.ca)

Frequently asked questions

How much money do I need for a Canada visitor visa?

IRCC does not publish a fixed amount. You must show enough money for your stay and return trip, with a normal account history. Large unexplained deposits hurt your case.

What is the super visa?

Parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens or permanent residents can apply for a super visa, which allows stays of up to 5 years per visit, with extra requirements such as medical insurance and an invitation from the child or grandchild.

Why are Canada visitor visas refused?

Common reasons are weak ties to your home country, unclear purpose, or finances that do not match the trip. You can reapply with stronger documents.
