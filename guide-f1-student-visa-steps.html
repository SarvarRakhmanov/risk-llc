F-1 US student visa: step by step

Last updated: 2026-10-11 · Checked Oct 2026 on official government websites.

Short answer

To get an F-1 visa you must first be accepted by a SEVP-certified US school, which issues your Form I-20. You then pay the $350 SEVIS I-901 fee, complete the DS-160, pay the $185 visa fee and attend an interview at the US Embassy in Tashkent. New students can get the visa up to 365 days before classes start but may enter the US no more than 30 days before the start date.

Step by step

  1. Choose and apply to SEVP-certified schools (check on Study in the States).
  2. Get admitted and receive your signed Form I-20 with your SEVIS ID.
  3. Pay the $350 SEVIS I-901 fee on the official ICE site.
  4. Complete the DS-160 and pay the $185 visa application fee.
  5. Schedule and attend the interview in Tashkent with your I-20 and financial documents.
  6. Enter the US no earlier than 30 days before your program start date.

Documents checklist

  • Passport valid at least 6 months beyond your stay
  • Form I-20 signed by you and the school
  • SEVIS I-901 fee receipt
  • DS-160 confirmation and visa fee receipt
  • Transcripts, diplomas and test scores (IELTS/TOEFL, SAT/GRE if required)
  • Proof of funds for tuition, living and travel costs (bank letters, sponsor letters)
  • Evidence of plans to return after studies

Fees (official)

  • SEVIS I-901 fee (F-1): $350
  • Visa application (MRV) fee: $185
  • Visa integrity fee: $250 under a 2025 law, collected at issuance where implemented; confirm with the Embassy

Official sources

  • Student visa (travel.state.gov) (travel.state.gov)

Frequently asked questions

Can I work on an F-1 visa?

Only in limited ways: on-campus jobs within limits, and practical training such as CPT or OPT with authorization. Unauthorized work breaks your status.

Can I study on a tourist visa instead?

No. Study toward a US degree or certificate is not allowed on a B visa, even for a short program.

Do my spouse and children need visas?

Yes, they apply for F-2 visas with their own I-20s from your school; they do not pay the SEVIS fee.
