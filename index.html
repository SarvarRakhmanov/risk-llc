FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No one can guarantee approval; only the government decides. We make sure your application is complete, consistent and well prepared.

Do you help with US B1/B2 visa interviews?

Yes. We fill the DS-160, book the appointment, prepare documents and run mock interviews in English, Russian or Uzbek.

Is the DV lottery 2027 entry free?

Yes. The official entry at dvprogram.state.gov is free. We help with the photo and entry and, if you are selected, the full green card process.

Can you help with Canada PR through Express Entry?

Yes. We assess your CRS score, plan language tests and ECA, create your profile and prepare the PR application after an invitation.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Russian (русский) and Uzbek (o‘zbek tili).
