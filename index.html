About RISK LLC

RISK LLC is a limited liability company registered in California, USA, founded by Sarvar Rakhmanov. We focus on clear, honest, practical guidance for people planning to study, work, visit or immigrate to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. Consultations are available in English, Russian and Uzbek.

Disclaimer: RISK LLC is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal representation. We are not affiliated with any government. Final decisions on all applications are made solely by the relevant government authorities, and no outcome is guaranteed. For legal matters, consult a licensed immigration attorney or authorized representative.

FAQ

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No. Only government authorities decide applications. We help you prepare a complete, accurate application.

Are you lawyers?

No. RISK LLC is a consulting company, not a law firm. We refer complex legal cases to licensed professionals.

Is the DV lottery entry free?

Yes. Entering the Diversity Visa program on the official U.S. Department of State website is free. Our fee covers optional guidance only.
